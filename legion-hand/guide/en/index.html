LEGION HAND · HOW TO PLAY

Legion Hand

Make poker hands. Let creatures blow up the score.
A card roguelike where poker hands attack and creature combos multiply the damage — not an RPG where you grind levels.

  • One attempt is called a run. Lose and you start over — but going further unlocks new decks.
  • Three things to know: hands · Troops × Mult · creatures.
  • The in-game tutorial walks you through your first battle. This page is the big picture.

1 · A run

6 gates, 18 battles, up to the Obsidian King

Each gate has three battles: Patrol → Elite → Gatekeeper. Win a battle, earn gold, visit the shop to grow your legion, then march on. Lose a single battle and the run ends.

1Pick a realm
2Win: slot +1
3Pick a realm
4Win: slot +1
5Realm Lord
6Obsidian King

Small dot = Patrol · Elite, big dot = Gatekeeper

  • Pick a realm — at gates 1 and 3 you choose one of two realms. Its Gatekeeper guards the next two gates. Their name, troops and skills are all shown up front, so pick the one you can counter.
  • Fight or skip — Patrols and Elites can be skipped. Skipping gives no gold, but you get one reward instead (creature pack · upgrade pack · a free creature at the next shop · skill charge +2 · gold). Gatekeepers can't be skipped.
  • Settlement → shop — the battle reward, 1 gold per ♥ left, and interest (+1 per 5 gold held, up to +5) stack up line by line, then you go to the shop. Tap once to skip the animation.

Beat a Gatekeeper and it shows up as a creature in the next shop. Beating the gate 2 and gate 4 Gatekeepers each adds a creature slot (3 → 5).

2 · Battle

Build a hand, then deploy

Cards come in four faction colors (Wind · Water · Earth · Fire) numbered 1–13. You start with 8 cards in hand.

  • Pick 1–5 cards — as soon as you pick, you see the hand and its damage. The Suggest buttons pick the strongest combos in your current hand.
  • Deploy — play the picked cards to cut the enemy's troops. Each deploy uses one ♥. Your hand refills by the cards you played.
  • Regroup — discard the cards you don't want and draw new ones. It costs no ♥.

4 deploy ♥

Bring the enemy's troops to 0 before your ♥ run out and you win. If your last ♥ can't finish it, the run ends. Every ♥ left becomes gold.

3 regroups

Three per battle. Don't hold back when you're chasing a big hand.

Long-press a card to see it larger. The red strip under the enemy is what it will do next.

3 · Damage

Damage = Troops × Mult

The blue number is Troops, the pink number is Mult, and the yellow number is the damage dealt. Harder hands have bigger base Troops and Mult.

Playing 7 · 7 · 7 — step by step

1
The hand sets the Troops. 7 · 7 · 7 is Three of a Kind. Card numbers 7 + 7 + 7 = 21, plus Three of a Kind's base 30 = 51 Troops.
51
2
The hand sets the Mult. Three of a Kind's Mult is ×3.
51×3=153
3
Creatures add Mult (+). Sea Dragon gives Mult +4 when you play 2+ cards of the same number. Mult 3 + 4 = 7.
51×7=357
4
Creatures multiply Mult (×). Dryad gives Mult ×1.2 when the hand has an Earth card. Mult 7 × 1.2 = 8.4.
51×8.4=428

Each creature adds Troops, adds Mult, or multiplies Mult. All "+" bonuses are added first, then "×" multiplies — so bringing adders and multipliers together makes damage snowball. Slot order doesn't matter, and cards outside the hand are discarded without scoring.

4 · Creatures

Your creature party grows your hands

The party starts with 3 slots and grows to 5. Your first run starts with no creatures — you buy your first one at the first shop. Tap a creature slot to open its details.

Trait — always on

Things like "Troops +12 per Wind card": when the condition is met, it adds Troops or Mult on its own when you deploy.

Skill — use when charged

Every deploy or regroup fills the charge bar by one. When it's full, a [Skill!] button lights up under the creature; tap it to see what the skill does, then confirm to use it. Skills swap cards, thaw frozen cards, power up the next deploy and more.

Roles — creatures that do more than score

Most creatures boost Troops or Mult, but role creatures change the battle itself. Theme packs in the shop gather one role together (there's a Strategist in every faction).

Evolution — creatures have stages Ⅰ · Ⅱ · Ⅲ. A creature evolves to Ⅱ after its trait fires enough times; Ⅲ only comes from an Evolution Stone or the material you get for beating a Realm Lord of the same faction. Evolving raises its numbers and shortens its skill charge.

5 · Enemies and Gatekeepers

Gatekeepers fight dirty

Enemies show their next action in advance. Patrols and Elites just have lots of troops, but Gatekeepers and the Obsidian King mess with your hand and party. Check them on the march map and bring creatures that counter them.

6 · Settlement and shop

Grow your legion between battles

You visit the shop after every win. Gold carries over to the next shop, and saving it earns interest.

Creatures

Four are offered each time; the first one matches a faction in your party. When your slots are full, the Release screen lets you pick one to let go — it shows the faction bonus you'd lose and the gold you get back (half its price) first.

Hand upgrades ★

Raise that hand's Troops and Mult for the rest of the run. Upgrade the hands you play most.

Theme packs

Creature packs built around one theme — Strategist, Wealth, Combat or a faction — plus upgrade packs. Open one to get three; tap one to read its ability, then Pick this. Creatures not yet in your Codex can appear.

Evolution Stones · boons

A stone evolves one creature by a stage. The boon is a creature slot +1. Hand, Regroup and Deploy +1 now come from role creatures.

Rerolls start at 5 gold (+1 each time). Interest is +1 per 5 gold held, up to +5.

7 · New players

Tips for your first few runs

Start with Suggest

Not sure which hand you have? Tap a Suggest button — it picks the strongest combo for you.

Collect one color

Two creatures of the same color turn on a faction bonus. Pick a color and gather its cards and creatures.

Grow one hand

Two Pair or Flush — pick a hand that comes up often and keep upgrading it in the shop. Damage jumps.

Scout the Gatekeeper

Tap the Gatekeeper's circle on the march map to check its skills. Freeze? Bring a thawing creature. Regenerate? Prepare a big burst.

Leftover ♥ = gold

Win fast and every ♥ left pays 1 gold. Keep gold in multiples of 5 for interest.

Don't hoard regroups

Regrouping costs no ♥. One card away from a big hand? Regroup for it.

Also

Unlock decks

Reaching gate 2 or gate 4 for the first time, or beating the Obsidian King, unlocks a new starting deck. The Short Deck, High Number Deck and Ember Deck each play differently.

Daily Challenge

Everyone gets the same conditions (same seed) that day. Your first attempt is saved in My Records.

Endless march

After beating the Obsidian King you can keep going from gate 7. How far can you get?

Codex

Fills up with every creature you meet. Creatures you first meet in theme packs also appear in the shop from your next run.
